# mai-salo-product-images

Produce a faithful photographic image and a traceable autonomous QA verdict. Route by the original background content, not camera angle or a `product_only` versus `model_worn` label. Catalog covers product-only views on plain or solid backgrounds. Contextual/lifestyle covers any textured fabric, surface, or setting, including a textured stepped surface, product-only still life, isometric or oblique views, and all model or worn images. An oblique product on a solid background stays catalog; a product on fabric stays contextual even without a person. Describe only observed surface properties; never invent a material or call every texture fabric. Record the route and source evidence before processing. If content is unclear, inspect the source instead of guessing from metadata.

Every applicable gate (product identity, metal material, gemstone realism, background, lighting, framing, human realism) must pass on the same final file; a pleasing image is not enough. The agent selects references, generates, checks, and retries without per-image human aesthetic approval. This is an agent-run procedure, not a deployed background service or a guarantee of exact image preservation. Photographic polish never permits a false product fact.

## Establish the run

1. Read Mai Salo's current project context and the owning task. Confirm whether the request is calibration, sample generation, batch production, or QA only. Authoring or loading this skill does not authorize generating images, spending money, scheduling work, or changing the store.
2. Retain the original source photographs, product and variant identity, available dimensions and weight with units and provenance, product type, material and finish, intended pose, and intended output surface. Retrieve existing catalog data before asking the user for it again. Inspect source resolution and identifying construction details before generating. A style reference is never evidence of product design.
3. Load the frozen reference pack and its version. It sets the rendering target for each alloy and finish, the catalog background, lighting/shadow and framing anchors, and each contextual source scene's preservation and lighting constraints. Read [calibration and measurement](references/calibration-and-measurement.md) before establishing or changing these targets, or when evidence is absent, stale, or contradictory. The agent may select targets from existing authorized source/style references. Freeze files, targets, and provenance before generation; never tune them afterward to make a failed output pass. In calibration, derive catalog framing from available product data and category context instead of requiring every physical measurement first. A target selected by AI is a rendering reference, not verified physical alloy color.
4. Before starting, record the exit condition: all required gates pass on the final file, or stop with the failed and unverified gates plus the smallest next action. Set a finite retry and cost limit. Unless the user sets a smaller limit, allow at most three generation attempts per product in one run, including the initial attempt. This is a safety ceiling, not permission to exceed an approved budget. Stop sooner if the same defect recurs without improvement, access fails, or a required input is missing. At a ceiling or recurring non-improvement, stop with the retained attempts and explicit verdicts instead of accepting the least-bad output. Continuing needs a new explicit review decision, not a reset counter.

Keep one non-resetting attempt ledger across feedback, restarts, track changes, and revised briefs within the same authorized work. A brief or QA-only revision authorizes zero new generations. Existing batch20's generation cap is exhausted; retain its prior counters and outputs without rerunning it. Only explicit new authorization with a recorded additional budget permits further production, and it never erases the earlier ledger.

Before any generation, inspect the original at native resolution against its track's quality, fidelity, scene, lighting, framing, and human-realism requirements. If it already meets them, keep it unchanged instead of forcing AI retouching, and retain its original hash, source comparisons, per-gate verdicts, and unchanged-source decision. This consumes no generation attempt. Generate only for a documented photographic defect that warrants editing, with sufficient source evidence and separate remaining authorization.

Incomplete measurements do not block a plausible visual representation, and missing nonessential measurements do not pause the run or trigger blind retries, when original photographs and usable product context support an honest visual estimate. Record whether sizing is dimension-supported or context-estimated, and which observations support it. A visual-plausibility pass does not verify missing physical dimensions. The absence of a human-approved gold master does not block AI reference selection or autonomous visual QA within the established style. Do not turn routine aesthetic or calibration decisions into approval requests. Pause only when evidence is too weak or contradictory to depict the item responsibly, a required check cannot be completed, or a limit is reached. Never relabel a failed or unverified required gate as passed or accepted, whether to avoid a needed correction or to finish without a human.

## Fix the visual contract

1. In both tracks, use restrained photographic retouching. Preserve fine surface character and credible material reflections. Aim for professional studio-quality photography, not flat yellow plastic, orange saturation, uniformly glossy CGI, invented scratches, or decorative sparkle. Never infer precise gold color from karat alone or force different alloys to one color.
2. For catalog only, generate the finished background directly in the approved lighter, near-white, low-chroma warm olive-gray direction, with a gentle vertical `olive-200 → olive-100` gradient. Add no transparency extraction, blue or green screen, texture, horizon, halo, vignette, or background-color spill on gold. Use one neutral-white soft studio lighting family across catalog peers, with broad soft highlights and controlled darker reflection bands. Freeze key-light direction, shadow color, contact strength, penumbra softness, and footprint relative to subject bounds. Shadows share treatment, not identical shapes or pixel sizes. A suspended chain has no floor-contact shadow; a resting ring may have a faint attached contact core and softer penumbra.
3. For contextual/lifestyle, preserve the existing scene, texture, background, crop, view, and pose. Never remove or replace the background, move the piece to the catalog plate, or invent a new setting. Improve the jewelry and the whole image with coherent professional studio-quality lighting, credible materials, reflections, and shadows consistent with the retained scene. Never polish only the jewelry while the surrounding light stays incoherent, and never force catalog light direction, neutrality, or shadow rules onto existing context.
4. In both tracks, preserve exact product identity: silhouette, band thickness, internal proportions, link topology and openings, joins, clasp, and stone outline, size, number, locations, cut, facets, prongs, and setting. Never resize a gemstone independently or alter any component to improve composition. Never invent hidden construction, complete a cropped chain, or substitute a prettier design. Inspect and disclose generated changes to reflections and microdetail.
5. Separate whole-product canvas framing from internal jewelry proportions. Catalog uses more generous, consistent canvas margins with evidence-led relative scale, not equal fill or strict shared physical scale. Scale only the whole product, uniformly, with its proportions unchanged. Full necklaces should read larger than rings while rings stay legible. Use dimensions, weight, product type, source proportions, pose, and applicable framing anchors. Weight cannot determine exact size, and total chain length is not image width. Establish a context-estimated anchor autonomously when evidence supports it, without inventing millimeters or universal category multipliers. Preserve native detail-crop roles: never complete missing construction or enlarge a detail into an equivalent overview.
6. Contextual framing preserves the native crop and a plausible product-size relationship to the body or setting. Never scale the piece uniformly to a catalog overview anchor. Keep catalog overviews square unless the surface requires otherwise, but never square-crop contextual images or detail views at the cost of their retained role.
7. For people, preserve identity, natural pose, skin pores, fine hair, creases, and tonal variation. Keep realistic lips, nails, anatomy and joints, natural hand positions, and credible jewelry contact. Remove a plasticky AI look without beauty-filter or wax skin, body or identity changes, extra or missing fingers, or invented poses. If a bad pose cannot be repaired faithfully from supported source evidence, mark the affected gate unverified or failed and stop acceptance instead of inventing anatomy.

## Require photographic gemstone realism

In both tracks, judge `gemstone_realism` separately from exact geometry and metal appearance. Before future authorized generation, select and freeze appropriate photographic gemstone-appearance references for the documented stone type, cut, size, and lighting, from reliable existing or supplier imagery where available, following [calibration and measurement](references/calibration-and-measurement.md). References govern optics and material only, never geometry, stone positions, prongs, or metal construction. Never claim physical gem identity from an image, treat an AI-generated neighbor as a real gemstone reference, or pass because an AI-looking source and candidate agree.

Assess facet-specific light/dark contrast, transparency, internal depth and refraction where visible, and credible localized highlights and dispersion when the light allows. Distinguish diamond, cubic zirconia, amethyst, and other colored stones only by actual source facts. Impose no universal CZ-always-shinier comparison, rainbow quota, starbursts, or identical shine. Reject opaque gel or molded plastic, flat pasted facets, uniform milky/chalk fill, over-smoothed continuous faces, CGI gloss, and decorative sparkle. More brightness or sharpening alone does not fix optics.

If source or candidate gem detail is unreadable, or photographic reference evidence is insufficient, mark `gemstone_realism` unverified, not passed. Without gems, inspect and retain reviewed not applicable with source evidence. A failed or unverified gemstone gate blocks acceptance even when gold and geometry pass. Earlier broad material passes do not verify gemstone quality until a specific recheck. References found now for existing outputs are diagnostic and dated as such; never pretend they were frozen before those files were generated. This correction triggers no new image call or budget reset.

## Generate one candidate

Skip this section when the original passes the pre-generation review; keeping a suitable source unchanged is a valid outcome. Never invent defects or regenerate every image by default.

Use the host's authorized ChatGPT/OpenAI image-editing capability with the original source, the approved reference pack, and the explicit contract. Discover supported settings and model availability at runtime. Never guess a model identifier or send unsupported complexity or quality settings. If the capability is unavailable, retain the prepared prompt and report the blocker instead of switching provider or using a person's logged-in desktop.

Give each input one role: original product and scene authority, gold-tone reference, photographic gemstone-optics-only reference, catalog-only background plate, or applicable framing reference. In the full prompt, specify the track and routing evidence, retained crop/pose, identity-critical details, whole-image lighting constraints, sizing basis, and output size. Add the human-preservation constraints when people are visible and the gemstone-realism contract when gems are present. Supply weight and product context without presenting them as exact dimensions. The catalog background plate donates no lighting or product and never replaces a contextual background. Style images donate no stones, branding, construction, people, or jewelry.

Retain each complete exact prompt, every input and output file hash, source and reference roles and hashes, the frozen pack version, tool and supported settings, the job identifier when exposed, the original generated file, the attempt ledger, and remaining limits. Never call a generated result an unchanged photograph or a pixel-preserving edit. Keep rejected candidates separate from accepted deliverables.

## QA the actual file

After generation completes, use `verify` to inspect the native-resolution file, matched source-detail crops, and a desktop and mobile product-grid preview. Use `artifacts` to assemble comparisons. Helper calls return their evidence here, not another full workflow. If a required helper is unavailable, report the missing check. Use `analyze` before reporting numeric measurements; compute them from retained pixels and product data, never from an unsupported model estimate.

Display and retain each gate separately with the exact file hash, reference-pack version, discrete comparison evidence, and reason. Use passed, failed, or unverified for applicable gates. Use reviewed not applicable only for an inapplicable track-specific gate, stating why and the source inspection behind it; the absence of a verdict is not a pass. Never average gates into a score or let a strong background excuse a changed stone setting.

| --- | --- |
| Exact product identity | Both tracks. Compare original and candidate at native resolution and in enlarged matched details: stone size, number, locations, facets and prongs, band thickness, silhouette, rails, links, openings, clasp, and connections. Reject added, lost, merged, independently resized, or reshaped components. Unreadable source detail is unverified, not permission to invent it. |
| Gold tone and material realism | Both tracks. Compare frozen alloy/finish targets and applicable peers under the same viewing conditions, separating midtones, specular highlights, and dark reflections. Contextual reflections must fit the retained scene light, not a forced catalog palette. Reject unexplained drift, plasticky shading, fabricated texture, and exaggerated sparkle. Retain visual comparisons and meaningful pixel diagnostics. A rendering match neither certifies physical alloy color nor passes the gemstone gate. |
| Gemstone realism (`gemstone_realism`) | Both tracks. Retain native matched source/candidate gem crops and the photographic optics references with provenance, hashes, and freeze or diagnostic date. Inspect each optical criterion and rejection condition in the gemstone section separately, without changing geometry. Apply that section's unverified, not-applicable, and blocking rules. |
| Catalog background consistency | Catalog only. Compare the frozen plate and neighboring catalog cards at equal render size. Measure fixed clean regions for lightness, undertone, and gradient. Reject unwanted casts, whole-frame darkening, and filled openings. Without a calibrated tolerance, numeric acceptance stays unverified. Contextual: reviewed not applicable with routing evidence, not a replacement-background pass. |

Catalog: reviewed not applicable with plain-background routing evidence. |

Retain the inspected separation and local-background estimates for measurements. Reject dark puddles, detached shadows, broad halos, or invented floor contact on suspended pieces. Contextual: assess physical shadow and contact quality against its scene-specific profile, not catalog uniformity. |

Retain full-image and jewelry-detail comparisons showing coherent professional photographic lighting across jewelry, people, fabric, and setting, with credible reflections, material response, contact, and cast shadows. Visual review stays necessary even when metrics pass. Image QA cannot certify exact geometry or physical color beyond the available evidence. Recheck all gates after any generation, resize, crop, color adjustment, export, or other change to the deliverable; only evidence for unchanged bytes stays current.

## Route failure and retry

1. A correctable failed gate triggers another generation attempt within the run's remaining limits. State the observed defect and the targeted correction. Regenerate from the original photograph and the same approved reference pack, never from a sequence of generated edits. A failed output may illustrate a defect but never becomes the product authority.
2. Keep unrelated constraints fixed and rerun every gate on the new candidate. Never weaken a reference or tolerance to make an image pass. A framing-only correction must not distort the product or silently change the agreed pose.
3. Correctable material, applicable background, scene-lighting, shadow, or human-realism defects get targeted retries only within existing authorization and remaining limits. Unreadable identity-critical details, unsupported pose repair, unusable evidence, or missing tools leave the affected checks failed or unverified.
4. A changed gold master, background plate, framing rule, or source product invalidates affected earlier acceptance. Recheck impacted images before including them in a batch. Never let a newer output become the next image's reference and gradually drift the catalog.
