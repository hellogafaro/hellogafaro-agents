# Calibration and measurement

Read this when establishing or changing a reference pack, when a product has no applicable framing anchor, or when required source data or acceptance tolerances are missing.

Inspect each original before authorizing any generation. If it already meets the applicable track's photographic quality and fidelity gates, keep its bytes unchanged and retain its hash, per-gate review and unchanged-source decision. Do not force retouching, spend an attempt or manufacture a defect to justify AI processing. A textured stepped surface is contextual even without fabric or people. Preserve what is visible and describe texture and geometry without inventing a specific material.

## Build a frozen reference pack

1. Resolve real product photographs and available measurements, weight and category information for representative pieces. Use existing supplier and catalog records before requesting more data. Start with a ring and a necklace, adding an earring or bracelet anchor before those categories enter a batch. Partial dimensions or weight plus product context can support a proposed framing anchor. Different materials and finishes need applicable references, not an assumed universal gold swatch.
2. Prefer an existing authoritative gold photograph for each alloy and finish. If none is established, the agent selects one rendering target from the authorized product/style references within the chosen visual direction. Record it as AI-selected, not human-approved or physically certified. A neutral, known lighting reference is better than trying to infer physical color from an uncalibrated supplier JPEG. Use a shared material crop or palette reference across comparable products rather than allowing each generation to invent its own gold.
3. Freeze that file, crop coordinates and hash before generation. Evaluate gold midtones, white reflections and dark bands separately, then compare the generated pair. Iterate failures autonomously while keeping the reference fixed. Do not ask the user to choose routine hue corrections, and do not promote a convenient output to the target after seeing it.
4. Inspect a mixed-category grid at desktop and mobile card sizes. The agent establishes the balance between real-life size cues and legibility using available evidence and proposed anchors. Identify dimension-supported and context-estimated anchors without requiring every piece to have complete measurements. Keep internal proportions faithful and make a full necklace read larger than a ring without rendering rings unusably small. Retain the comparison and verdict rather than treating an internal preference as physical proof.
5. Record a pack version, selection or existing approval provenance, immutable reference files and hashes, track and routing evidence, product and pose coverage, applicable lighting and shadow rules, framing targets with units, their dimension-supported or context-estimated basis, measurement method and any frozen numeric acceptance ranges. For contextual images, freeze the original full scene and relevant texture, crop, jewelry and human-detail references, scene-specific lighting constraints and preservation criteria. Do not supply the catalog plate as a replacement or apply catalog overview anchors to people or settings. Keep the pack with the task or established asset library, not only in memory. Updating it invalidates affected prior checks and must not excuse failure.

## Freeze photographic gemstone references

Apply `gemstone_realism` independently in both tracks, not inside gold appearance or exact product geometry. Before future authorized generation, select and freeze photographic gemstone-appearance references appropriate to the documented stone type, cut, size and lighting. Prefer reliable existing or supplier photographs where available and inspect whether they actually show useful gem detail. Record source provenance, documented product facts and uncertainties, file hashes, crop coordinates, pack version and freeze date. Diamond, cubic zirconia, amethyst and other colored-stone expectations follow actual source facts, not guessed visual identity. An image cannot certify physical gem identity. If the evidence cannot support appropriate photographic comparison, leave the gate unverified rather than substitute an arbitrary stone.

These references supply optics and material appearance ONLY. They must not donate geometry, stone outline, size, count or positions, cut, prongs, setting or metal construction. Preserve those against the actual product authority. An AI-generated neighbor is not an actual photographic gemstone reference. Agreement between an AI-looking source and an AI-looking candidate does not establish realism. Previously generated calibration samples above remain catalog-background references, not gemstone-appearance authorities.

At native resolution, retain matched source/candidate gem crops and reference crops that separately show facet-specific light/dark contrast, visible transparency, internal depth and refraction, credible localized highlight response, and dispersion when supported by the scene's light. Do not require effects the view or lighting cannot reveal. Do not impose a universal CZ-always-shinier rule, rainbow quota, starbursts or identical shine across facets or stones. Reject opaque gel or molded plastic, flat pasted facets, uniform milky/chalk fill, over-smoothed continuous faces, CGI gloss and decorative sparkle. Extra brightness or sharpening alone cannot repair implausible optics. Keep exact geometry and photographic optics as separate verdicts.

Unreadable source or candidate gem detail, or insufficient photographic reference evidence, makes `gemstone_realism` unverified, never passed by source agreement. No gems requires explicit source inspection and a reviewed not-applicable verdict with evidence. Display and retain this gate, its reason and discrete crop/reference evidence. Failed or unverified required gemstone realism blocks acceptance even when geometry and gold pass. Earlier broad material passes are invalid for the disputed gemstone quality until a specific recheck, not evidence of a new pass.

For already generated files, any newly found reference is a dated diagnostic reference, not a retroactive pre-generation freeze. Record this distinction in the review and reference pack. Freeze appropriate references before separately authorized future production. A QA correction authorizes no image calls, does not replenish exhausted budgets, and never erases prior attempt counters.

## Interpret dimensions without inventing them

Keep supplied dimensions, derived values and visual estimates separate. Retain the selected variant and units, and calculate conversions with code. Use a pictured or documented representative variant where available. If a range has no specified representative, a calibration proposal may use a clearly disclosed representative choice; do not invent a literal average or claim it describes an actual variant. Ask only if the choice materially changes the result.

1. For a ring, distinguish size-system designation, inside diameter or circumference, outside dimensions and band or setting thickness. An inside diameter is not automatically its outside footprint. Use verified conversion data only when required.
2. For a necklace or bracelet, distinguish total wearable length, link width and thickness, pendant dimensions and the visible laid-out or hanging footprint. A U-shaped drape's image width cannot be calculated from total chain length alone. Use a measured pose, a dimensioned source reference or an explicitly approved visual anchor with sizing uncertainty recorded.
3. For earrings, distinguish a single piece from a pair, outside hoop diameter, drop length, width and stud dimensions. Pair spacing is layout, not product size. Compare single-piece dimensions while keeping the approved pair presentation.
4. Preserve the original view and intentional crop unless there is authorization and adequate source information to change them. A chain exiting the source frame must not acquire an invented clasp. Its existing crop can support context-estimated framing without claiming that the image establishes the entire chain's dimensions.

For catalog overviews, use an anchor sheet to define visible whole-product width and height as fractions of the canvas, with more generous consistent margins, pose and crop noted. Scale the entire piece uniformly; stone size, stone count and locations, prongs, band thickness, clasp, links and all internal proportions remain fixed. Relative scale compresses size differences for readability, not a physical ruler. Establish an evidence-supported anchor autonomously when none exists. Do not hard-code universal category percentages or claim physical scale across tiles. Known measurements constrain presentation; context never invents product facts. Check neighboring catalog products rather than equal fill. Preserve native detail crops as details, not overviews. Contextual images retain source crop and plausible relationships to body and setting, without uniform scaling to these anchors.

## Use weight and item type for plausibility

Retain the weight's units, variant and whether it describes a single piece, a pair or the whole item. Weight, item type and a source photograph can support a useful visual estimate even without a complete dimension set. Use the source silhouette and construction to distinguish a delicate chain, a broad band, a hollow hoop or a stone-set piece. Compare with approved pieces of similar material and construction when available.

Do not convert weight directly into diameter, chain length or band thickness. Hollow construction, stone weight, alloy and hidden geometry make that inference underdetermined. More grams do not automatically mean a wider or longer item. Never add bulk, enlarge stones or change link thickness merely to make a weight seem plausible.

Apply ordinary human-use scale constraints before optimizing composition. A wearable ring cannot plausibly have necklace-scale diameter. Flag conflicting units or a length mistaken for diameter, preserve the supplied record and resolve the interpretation rather than silently rewriting it. Do not demand a supplier answer for every absent axis when the photograph and context already support a credible representation.

Record the sizing basis as dimension-supported, context-estimated or insufficient evidence. Context-estimated framing can pass AI visual QA after inspection against the source and the mixed-product grid. Exact physical scale remains unverified unless actually established. Insufficient evidence still requires a pause; the presence of a number alone is not enough if it refers to the wrong variant or feature.

## Freeze consistent lighting and shadow treatment

For catalog, use one lighting setup per image family, with key direction, fill and white balance recorded. Distinguish contact, suspended and intentionally cropped poses. A hanging necklace has no floor-contact shadow, while a resting ring has an attached localized one. Consistency means compatible lighting and shared shadow treatment, not an identical ellipse pasted below every object. For contextual images, use a source-scene-specific profile: improve the piece AND the whole image to coherent professional studio-quality lighting, with material reflections and shadows consistent with retained people, fabric and setting. Preserve scene structure and texture. Do not impose catalog light direction, white balance or background neutrality. Retain full-scene and detail comparisons for a separate scene-lighting gate.

Record shadow strength relative to the adjacent clean background, footprint width and depth relative to subject bounds, hue neutrality, contact attachment and edge softness. Inspect a narrow contact core separately from the broader penumbra; a tiny dark contact point does not justify a wide dark puddle. Define these targets before generating, using the style reference and physically plausible object pose. Do not change targets just because the candidate misses them.

Measure shadows only after inspecting the product mask and local-background estimate. Product reflections and gold pixels must not be counted as shadow. A gradient plate can help estimate the clean background, but correct for local generated-background drift before interpreting a difference as cast shadow. Retain masks or marked regions so another run can reproduce the check. Compare neighboring products after normalizing shadow extent to product size.

AI visual QA must reject incompatible direction, excessive darkness or footprint, hard detached edges, global darkening and invented contact shadows on suspended pieces. Evaluate gold, applicable background and shadow checks separately and together. A close catalog background cannot compensate for orange gold or heavy shadow. For contextual images, retain a separate background-preservation verdict supported by full-scene and texture crops, not catalog-plate measurements. Retry from the original source and frozen applicable profile only if authorized budget remains.

## Calibrate repeatable QA

Use `analyze` for pixel measurements and dimension calculations, with retained inputs and code. Read image color profiles and document comparison color space. For catalog, sample the same normalized clean-background regions across candidates and plate, excluding product pixels, antialiasing and local shadows. Include top, middle and bottom regions and readable jewelry openings. Record obstructed regions and alternatives; never choose whichever patch passes. Do not treat textured contextual scenes as clean catalog regions. Compare their preserved structure and texture, and assess scene-aware illumination changes without requiring unchanged pixel values.

Measure the subject bounds separately from its shadow, and retain an inspected outline or mask so its measurement is reproducible. A segmentation model's bounding box is not automatically ground truth. Check subject occupancy, position and intended crop against the approved framing sheet, then inspect the grid render because a numeric box cannot establish perceived scale.

The agent may establish operational acceptance ranges using the frozen reference, previously accepted style examples and visibly rejected examples. Record provenance, units and the exact method for background lightness and undertone, subject occupancy, shadow attenuation/extent and any gold-color comparison. Freeze thresholds before the generation being judged; do not tune them on that candidate to manufacture a pass. Gold patch metrics support, but do not replace, material and reflection review. No universal color-difference cutoff can establish an exact physical gold tone.

Until numeric ranges are calibrated, numeric acceptance remains unverified. The agent can still make and retain a separate AI visual-consistency verdict against the frozen gold, background, lighting and shadow references and the mixed-product grid. Initial framing anchors can be established during calibration. Do not mislabel a selected operational threshold as physical validation. Routine aesthetic QA does not wait for human approval, but insufficient evidence for a required fidelity check still prevents acceptance. Autonomous means the agent owns decisions and bounded retries, not that every output must be declared successful.

## Retain a per-attempt QA record

Record product and variant identity, source-background track and its evidence, original sources and hashes, dimension and weight provenance, sizing basis, frozen pack version and provenance, full exact prompt and every input role/hash, non-resetting attempt ledger and remaining limits, output hash and exposed job identifier. For every applicable gate retain passed, failed or unverified, reason, method and a discrete comparison file or crop. Display gemstone realism, background preservation, scene lighting and human realism separately in the review and retained record, not hidden inside one aesthetic score. Keep photographic gemstone references, their source facts and hashes, and pre-generation freeze versus later diagnostic dates explicit. Include applicable peer consistency and dedicated shadow results, actual native source/candidate comparisons, matched detail crops and desktop/mobile preview captures. These are requirements for future authorized production, not evidence that a brief-only revision ran image QA. Keep exact physical-scale verification, human approval and publication state separate.

For human realism inspect full pose and native matched skin, hair, face, lips, nails, hands, joints and jewelry contact. Preserve identity, body and natural pose, skin pores, fine hair, creases and tonal variation. Reject wax skin, beauty-filter smoothing, plasticky AI anatomy, extra or missing fingers, invented poses and implausible contact. A bad pose without evidence for faithful repair stays failed or unverified; do not invent a replacement. With no people, retain an explicitly inspected reviewed not-applicable verdict and its full-scene evidence, never an automatic pass. Apply exact product identity to both tracks, including stone size/count/locations/prongs, band thickness, clasp and links. Photographic polish cannot authorize product redesign.

For a retry, record the failed gate and targeted correction before the next call. For a stop, record whether inputs, access, budget, non-improvement or ceiling caused it. Preserve rejected outputs separately. Feedback, restarts, route changes and brief revisions do not reset counters or authorize new generations. Brief/QA-only work permits zero new generations; existing batch20's cap remains exhausted and its prior ledger stays intact. Further production requires explicit additional authorization and budget without erasing history. An agent's confidence statement is not a substitute for the underlying comparison.
