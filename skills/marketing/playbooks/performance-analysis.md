### Performance analysis

**You own a conclusion the reader can act on, with its limits beside it.**

1. Record the intake through `tasks` when this is accepted work. State the question, the brand, the period, the comparison period, and the decision the answer feeds.
2. Pull the data through the host's integrations from every relevant source, using the brand's accounts from its project context: Shopify for revenue and orders, ad platforms for spend and attributed results, GA4 or PostHog for sessions and conversion, Klaviyo for owned-channel results, Search Console for organic. Query month by month for long windows. Save every response to a file with provider, account, tool, window, and metric definition.
3. Compute through `analyze`: every figure from the saved data by script, definitions reconciled in code (attribution, time zone, currency, deduplication, coverage), totals tied, a key figure recomputed a second way, and the change decomposed by segment, product, channel, campaign, and date with contributions ranked and outliers flagged against variance.
4. Use `investigate` on the ranked drivers to find what changed operationally at that time (launches, budget changes, stockouts, site changes, tracking changes), with evidence.
5. Write the analysis through `reporting`: conclusion first, evidence with source and window, drivers, confidence, limitations beside the claims, and the recommended next action or decision. Use `artifacts` for a chart only when it makes the comparison clearer.
6. Verify through `verify`: the period and baseline are right, the numbers are internally consistent, every claim traces to retained data, and recommendations follow from the evidence.
7. Deliver through `deliver` to the task; stakeholder communication follows the approval rules.

**Reply:** the conclusion, the two or three numbers that carry it with source and window, the drivers, the confidence, and the next action.
