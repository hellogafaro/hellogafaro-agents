### Campaign brief

**You own a brief someone can execute without asking what you meant.**

1. Record the intake through `tasks-operations`. Capture the goal in business terms, the period, the budget or constraint, and any fixed elements (dates, products, offers, channels already chosen).
2. Ground in the brand: read its Notion project context, past campaign results, and current audience and product performance through the host's integrations on the brand's accounts (Shopify for products and customers, GA4 or PostHog for behavior, Klaviyo for lists and segments, ad platforms for historical CPA and ROAS).
3. Use `research` for external facts that change the plan: seasonality, competitor offers, platform policy, or format changes, with citations.
4. Settle the open choices through `brainstorm` with concrete options: audience, offer, primary channel mix, budget split, creative direction, success metric. Present two or three real options with the tradeoff, not a questionnaire.
5. Write the brief through `write`: objective and success metric with its measurement definition; audience and segments; offer and messaging pillars; channel plan with budget and schedule; creative requirements per channel; tracking and naming conventions; risks and dependencies; decision owner and approval points.
6. Verify through `verify` that every number in the brief was computed through `analyze` and traces to a source and window, every channel has an account connected, and the tracking plan can actually be measured on the available data.
7. Store the brief through `documents-operations` in the brand's project and link it from the task. Deliver through `deliver` with the approval needed to start execution.

**Reply:** the objective and success metric, the recommended plan in a short table, the decisions still open, and the link to the brief.
